# Claims Process and Service Commitments (illustrative)

> Fictional content for demo purposes.

## 1. How to file a claim
1. Notify the company within 30 days of the accident by phone, email, or through your agent.
2. Submit the claim form with supporting documents.
3. The company acknowledges the claim within 1 working day and assigns a claims officer.

## 2. Documents for Personal Accident claims
- Accomplished claim form and valid government ID.
- Medical certificate or hospital records (for medical reimbursement and hospital income).
- Police report or barangay certification for accidents involving vehicles or assault.
- Death certificate and proof of relationship for death claims.
- Original official receipts for medical reimbursement.

## 3. Service commitments
- Complete documents are decided within 10 working days; payment is released within 3 working days of approval via bank transfer or GCash.
- Incomplete documents: the claims officer lists what is missing within 2 working days.
- Claims under PHP 20,000 with complete documents are eligible for fast-track processing.

## 4. Common reasons a claim is denied
Accident outside the policy period, an excluded activity, late notice without good reason, or missing proof of the accident.

## 5. Customer protection
Customers can escalate unresolved concerns to the Insurance Commission consumer assistance desk. Personal data collected is handled in line with the Data Privacy Act.
