# Home Protect Fire and Allied Perils (illustrative product sheet)

> Fictional product sheet for demo purposes.

## 1. Coverage
Fire, lightning, explosion, earthquake shock, typhoon, flood, and riot for the house structure and contents, up to the sum insured.

## 2. Premium
From PHP 1,800 a year for a PHP 1,000,000 sum insured on a standard residential property.

## 3. Exclusions
Intentional damage, theft without forced entry, war, nuclear risks, and properties used for storing flammable goods.

## 4. Claims
Notify within 7 days. Required: claim form, fire report from the Bureau of Fire Protection, photos, and proof of ownership.
