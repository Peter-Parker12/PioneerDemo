# Motor Car Comprehensive Insurance (illustrative product sheet)

> Fictional product sheet for demo purposes.

## 1. Coverage
- Own damage: accidental collision, fire, theft, and natural perils such as flood and typhoon (with the Acts of Nature extension).
- Third-party property damage and bodily injury: up to PHP 500,000 each.
- Personal accident for driver and passengers: PHP 50,000 per seat.
- Roadside assistance: towing up to 20 km, flat tire, battery jump.

## 2. Premium
Rates start at about 2.5% of the vehicle's sum insured per year. Deductible (participation) of PHP 3,000 per own-damage claim for vehicles up to PHP 1M sum insured.

## 3. Exclusions
Driving under the influence, unlicensed driver, use for racing, wear and tear, mechanical breakdown, and losses outside the Philippines.

## 4. Claims
Report within 30 days. Required: claim form, LTO OR/CR, driver's license, police report for collision or theft, repair estimate from an accredited shop. Own-damage claims up to PHP 50,000 with complete documents may be fast-tracked.
