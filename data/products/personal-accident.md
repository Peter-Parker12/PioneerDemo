# Family Protect Personal Accident Plan (illustrative product sheet)

> Fictional product sheet for demo purposes. Replace with Pioneer's actual product documents.

## 1. Who can be covered
- Principal member aged 18 to 65; spouse; children aged 3 months to 21 years (unmarried, dependent).
- Coverage renews yearly up to age 70 for principal member.

## 2. Plans and annual premium
| Plan | Accidental death / permanent disability | Accident medical reimbursement | Daily hospital income | Annual premium (family of 4) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | PHP 100,000 | PHP 10,000 | PHP 500 per day, up to 30 days | PHP 1,490 |
| Plus | PHP 250,000 | PHP 25,000 | PHP 1,000 per day, up to 30 days | PHP 2,990 |
| Max | PHP 500,000 | PHP 50,000 | PHP 1,500 per day, up to 45 days | PHP 5,490 |
Premium can be paid in full or quarterly (quarterly adds a 3% instalment charge). Plus works out to about PHP 8 a day for the whole family.

## 3. What is covered
- Death or permanent disability caused directly by an accident, worldwide, 24 hours a day.
- Accidents while riding as a driver or passenger of a motorcycle are covered, provided the rider holds a valid license and wears a helmet.
- Murder and assault, unprovoked.
- Burial benefit: PHP 10,000 added to the death benefit under Plus and Max.
- Child education benefit under Max: PHP 50,000 paid to the guardian if the principal member dies from an accident.

## 4. Exclusions
- Suicide or self-inflicted injury; illness or disease of any kind (not an accident).
- Participation in racing, professional sports, or hazardous activities such as skydiving or scuba diving beyond 30 metres.
- Injuries while under the influence of alcohol or illegal drugs.
- War, military operations, and riot in which the member is an active participant.
- Pre-existing physical defects or injuries.
- Motorcycle riders without a valid license or without a helmet.

## 5. How it works with PhilHealth and HMO
This plan pays a fixed cash benefit and reimburses accident medical costs on top of whatever PhilHealth or an HMO pays. It is not a replacement for either; benefits are not reduced because the member also has PhilHealth.

## 6. Waiting period
Accidents are covered from the effective date. There is no waiting period for accidents. Hospital income benefit starts after a 24-hour confinement.
